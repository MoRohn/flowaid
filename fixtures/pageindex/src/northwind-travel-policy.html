Northwind Travel and Expense Policy

This policy explains how the fictional company Northwind books travel and reimburses expenses. It is sample material written for FlowAId and may be redistributed freely. It applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business.

1. Booking travel

1.1 Air travel

Flights are booked through the company travel desk at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 8 hours, with approval from a director.

1.2 Rail and car

Rail is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Rental cars are booked in the compact category unless four or more employees travel together.

1.3 Hotels

Hotels are booked through the travel desk. The nightly limit is $180 in most cities and $260 in London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich.

2. Daily allowances

2.1 Meals

The meal allowance is $65 per day for domestic travel and $85 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

2.2 Incidentals

Laundry, tips and local transport are covered up to $25 per day. Receipts are required for any single item above $10.

3. Approvals

Every trip needs approval from the traveller's manager before booking. Trips above $5,000 in total also need approval from the finance department. International trips need approval from a director.

4. Expense claims

4.1 Deadlines

Expense claims are submitted within 30 days of returning. Claims submitted later than 60 days are not reimbursed.

4.2 Receipts

Receipts are uploaded as photos or PDF files with each claim. Lost receipts can be replaced by a signed declaration for items up to $75.

4.3 Reimbursement

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

5. Safety

Travellers register every international trip with the security team. In an emergency, travellers call the 24-hour assistance line printed on their travel card.

Appendix A. Frequently asked questions

Can I book business class for a 6-hour flight? Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours when a director approves.

Is the hotel limit the same everywhere? No. See section 1.3.

Who approves international trips? A director approves every international trip.
